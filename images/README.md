## Blade Analysis Summary

The project report compares three blade concepts:

| Blade alternative | Geometry | Maximum stress | Maximum contact pressure | Reported design observation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Propeller-shaped | 51.94 MPa | 51.12 MPa | High stress and contact pressure |
| 2 | Rectangular | 11.58 MPa | 10.91 MPa | Lower stress/pressure than Alternative 1, but uneven mixing |
| 3 | Helical | 8.88 MPa | 5.145 MPa | Lower reported stress/pressure and intended to mix evenly while pushing material toward the exit |

The report selected the helical concept on the basis of the reported structural results together with its intended mixing and material-transport function.

## Related Design Data

The project report documents:

- Waste design basis: 30 kg
- Calculated minimum shaft torque: 11 N·m
- Selected shaft diameter: 15 mm
- Calculated drive power: 138.21 W (0.19 HP)
- Selected motor: 0.25 HP
- Drum diameter: approximately 270 mm
- Estimated project cost: ₹17,592
